This document provides an operational workflow for handling requests under Thailand's PDPA. Rights are subject to statutory conditions, exceptions, and applicable procedures; verify the current legal requirements before implementation.

## 2. Rights That May Apply
Depending on the circumstances and statutory conditions, an individual may request:
- **Access and copy:** access to personal data and obtain a copy.
- **Rectification:** correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erasure or destruction:** deletion or destruction where legal conditions are met.
- **Restriction:** restriction of processing in specified circumstances.
- **Objection:** object to certain processing, including processing based on applicable legitimate interests or direct marketing.
- **Data portability:** receive or transmit certain data in the circumstances specified by law.
- **Consent withdrawal:** withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

Some requests may be refused or limited where a legal exception applies. Record the reason and communicate it appropriately.

## 3. Standard Workflow
1. **Receive:** log the request, date, channel, request type, and relevant account/system.
2. **Acknowledge:** confirm receipt without promising an outcome before assessment.
3. **Verify identity:** use proportionate verification; do not collect excessive identity documents.
4. **Clarify scope:** ask a focused question only if needed to locate the relevant data or understand the request.
5. **Assess:** identify the data, purpose, lawful basis, systems, processors, and any legal exception.
6. **Coordinate:** assign tasks to system owners and relevant processors.
7. **Respond:** provide the decision and action taken within the applicable statutory timeframe; document any lawful extension or refusal.
8. **Close:** record completion, evidence, and any follow-up controls.

## 5. Escalation
Escalate complex requests, potential legal exceptions, third-party rights issues, or requests involving sensitive data to the privacy lead or legal counsel.

## 6. Quality Controls
- Track statutory deadlines.
- Apply decisions consistently across relevant systems and processors.
- Preserve an audit trail.
- Restrict access to request records.
- Do not retain deleted data in active systems unless another lawful basis or legal obligation applies; manage backups according to a documented schedule.
