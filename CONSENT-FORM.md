# Consent Form and Withdrawal (Sample)

> Use this form only when consent is the appropriate lawful basis. Consent must be freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous, and the controller must be able to demonstrate it. Do not make optional consent a condition of core service access unless the processing is genuinely necessary and legally justified.

## A. Optional Marketing Consent
**Controller:** [Legal entity name]  
**Purpose:** To send information about products, services, events, or offers by [email / SMS / other channel].  
**Data used:** [Email address / telephone number / preference data].  
**Recipients:** [Controller and relevant communication service providers].  
**Retention:** Until consent is withdrawn or [defined period], subject to applicable legal requirements.

[ ] **I agree** to receive marketing communications from [Organization name] through the channels selected above.

Channels (optional):
- [ ] Email
- [ ] SMS
- [ ] Telephone

Do not pre-tick the box. Keep the choice separate from acceptance of general terms and other unrelated purposes.

## B. Optional Analytics / Product Improvement Consent
Use only if consent is the appropriate legal basis and the activity is not already justified by another applicable basis.

[ ] **I agree** to the use of [specified analytics technologies/data] for [clearly described purpose].

Explain the technologies, data categories, retention, and any third-party recipients before enabling them.

## C. Recording Evidence of Consent
For each consent event, record:
- User/account identifier (avoid unnecessary data)
- Exact notice and consent text version
- Purpose and selected channel(s)
- Date/time and collection method
- Affirmative action and status
- Withdrawal date/time, if applicable

Do not store more evidence than necessary. Protect consent records with access controls and an audit trail.

## D. Withdrawal
You may withdraw consent at any time using [account settings / unsubscribe link / privacy email]. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. After withdrawal, stop the processing that relied on that consent, subject to any other lawful basis or legal requirement that may apply.

**Withdrawal contact:** [privacy@example.com]

## E. Implementation Notes
- Use separate choices for separate purposes.
- Do not bundle marketing with account registration.
- Make withdrawal as easy as giving consent.
- Ensure withdrawal propagates to relevant systems and processors.
- Retain a minimal suppression record where necessary to honor an opt-out.
- Reassess the lawful basis if the purpose or processing changes.

**Template warning:** The sample wording must be adapted to the actual data, purpose, channel, and processing operation. Obtain legal review before use.
