# Privacy Notice at Collection (Sample)

**Controller:** [Legal entity name]  
**Service:** [Service name]  
**Notice date:** [DD Month YYYY]  
**Privacy contact:** [Email / address]

This notice should be presented at or before the point where personal data is collected, in a concise and accessible form. Provide additional detail through the full Privacy Policy.

| Item | Sample disclosure — customize before use |
|---|---|
| Data collected | Name, email, account details, device/log data, support messages |
| Source | Directly from the user; automatically from the device/service |
| Purposes | Account creation, service delivery, security, support, legal compliance |
| Lawful basis | Determine separately for each purpose: contract, legal obligation, legitimate interests, consent, or another applicable basis |
| Recipients | Hosting, email, support, analytics providers, and authorities where legally required; list actual categories |
| Retention | According to the documented retention schedule; specify actual periods or criteria |
| Mandatory fields | Identify fields necessary to provide the requested service and consequences of not providing them |
| Rights and contact | Explain applicable data-subject rights and provide a working privacy contact |
| International transfers | Identify destination and safeguards where applicable |

## Suggested Short Notice
"[Organization name] collects the information you provide (such as your name and email address) and certain technical logs to create and secure your account, provide the Service, and respond to support requests. We use the lawful basis applicable to each purpose, as described in our [Privacy Policy]. Some fields are required to provide the Service; if you do not provide them, we may be unable to create or maintain your account. For questions or to exercise your privacy rights, contact [privacy email]."

## Review Before Publication
- Confirm each data field and its source with the product and engineering teams.
- Confirm each purpose, lawful basis, recipient, and retention period.
- Identify any sensitive personal data, children’s data, profiling, cookies, or cross-border transfers and add tailored disclosures.
- Ensure the notice is accessible at every relevant collection point.
